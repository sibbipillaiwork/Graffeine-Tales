The Graffeine Chronicle

The Story Behind Graffeine Tales

Born from events. Brewed with coffee. Made with art.

✦ Graffeine Tales Atelier — Where stories, coffee notes, and tactile craftsmanship collide.

It started with events. Then came design. And eventually, design became something you could hold.

My journey began in event planning, amidst tight deadlines, endless checklists, and the beautiful chaos of bringing ideas to life. Somewhere along the way, I found myself drawn to the art of designing experiences and creating moments worth remembering.

The days were hectic, the coffee was endless, and creativity kept brewing. What began as event planning slowly evolved into event design, graphic design, and a desire to create something more personal.

I wanted my designs to live beyond screens and event spaces — to become things people could hold, gift, use, and cherish.

And so, Graffeine Tales was born: a blend of graphics and caffeine, fuelled by a love for art, handmade details, and the little things that bring joy.

Today, from illustrated stationery and personalised gifts to thread art and handmade keepsakes, every creation is a mix of thoughtful design and a little creative magic.

Because every event, mood, and moment deserves a little art, a little joy, and a good dose of caffeine.

Graffeine Tales — where creativity is brewed and stories are made tangible.
