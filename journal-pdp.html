Frequently Asked Questions

Have a question about paper weights, nib compatibility, or gift boxes? We've got you covered.

Zero feathering and zero bleed-through. We use 100 GSM premium acid-free natural paper specifically selected and laboratory-tested with fine, medium, and broad fountain pen nibs (Pilot Kakuno, Lamy Safari, Pelikan 4001 inks) as well as Zebra Mildliners and Tombow Dual Brush markers.

The Dated 2027 Edition comes with all days, dates, and Indian national calendar festivals pre-printed from January 1 to December 31, 2027. The Undated Edition gives you 365 open daily and weekly spreads that you date yourself, making it ideal if you start mid-year or take journaling breaks without leaving empty wasted sheets.

The sticker sheets come nestled neatly inside the rear expandable gusset pocket. They are loose sheets on a protective backing so you can remove them, arrange them on your desk, and stick them wherever you like without tearing out pages from your journal.

Yes! During checkout in the Cart drawer, you can add an optional gift message. Our studio calligrapher will handwrite your note on textured archival paper and seal it inside the box with a golden wax seal at no additional charge.

Unlike cheap glued "perfect bound" notebooks where pages fall out after a few months, Smyth Sewn binding physically stitches signatures of folded pages with high-tensile cotton thread before adhering them to the cloth spine. It is the gold standard of heirloom bookbinding and is guaranteed to stay intact for decades.
